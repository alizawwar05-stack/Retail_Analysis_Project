CONTEXT MATTERS

About this analysis

ONLINE RETAIL

What counts as a sale?

Positive quantities and prices, excluding invoices beginning with C. Sales include postage and manual entries except in the product ranking. Amounts are in GBP, before credit deductions.

How are customers counted?

Distinct recorded CustomerIDs only. Repeat customers have more than one distinct sales invoice within the selected dates and country. Anonymous sales remain in overall totals.

What are the limits?

Exact duplicate rows were removed during cleaning. Partial months should not be compared as full months. High sales do not prove high profit; costs and linked returns are not available.
